Hardware limits for local AI? Fixed.

Run a 27B open model on the free GPUs that Kaggle and Colab give every account, and chat with it
from your browser. The full guide is README.md in this folder, or github.com/zamsonl/no-gpu-local-ai

What's in here
  notebooks/kaggle.ipynb   the server, for Kaggle (recommended: two free GPUs)
  notebooks/colab.ipynb    the same server, for Colab (one free GPU)
  web/index.html           the chat app: double-click it to open it in your browser
  cli/chat.mjs             optional chat in the terminal (needs Node 18 or newer)

Quick start on Kaggle
  1. Sign in to kaggle.com and verify your phone number (Settings > Phone verification).
     Kaggle's phone verification often fails. If no code arrives, or it says "too many requests",
     stop retrying and ask Kaggle to verify you through its help form: https://www.kaggle.com/contact
  2. Create > New Notebook, then File > Import Notebook, and upload notebooks/kaggle.ipynb.
  3. In the panel on the right, Settings: Accelerator = GPU T4 x2, and turn Internet on.
  4. Press Run All. After four to six minutes the output says READY.
  5. Click "Open the chat". Or copy the link it prints, open web/index.html and paste it there.
  6. When you're done: the ... menu (top right) > Shut the server down, so the notebook stops
     using your 30 GPU hours a week.

On Colab instead: upload notebooks/colab.ipynb at colab.research.google.com, choose
Runtime > Change runtime type > T4 GPU, then Runtime > Run all, and keep the tab open.

Your chats
  The chat app saves your chats in your browser, so they're still there after you close it.
  They don't follow you to another device: Settings > Your chats can export and import them.
  The terminal chat keeps its chats in a .no-gpu-local-ai folder in your home folder.
  The notebook keeps nothing and forgets everything when its session ends.

Keep the link to yourself: anyone with it can use your server until it stops.
